# Notes for the AI transparency statement and the privacy policy: Citizen Services-style lab

> **DRAFT NOTES FOR A LAB.** Under the national government AI policy an agency publishes a transparency statement about its AI use. From 10 December 2026 the Privacy Act also asks an entity to say in its privacy policy about certain automated decisions. These notes are plain-language raw material, not legal text. Communications, privacy and legal would write the real wording.

## What to say about the assistant (draft, plain language)

- "When you use our online chat or phone menu you may talk to an automated assistant. It will tell you it is an assistant."
- "It gives general information from content we have approved. It does not make decisions about you, and it cannot tell you whether you are eligible for anything."
- "It will not give migration advice, predict the outcome of an application, or ask for your passport number or other identity details."
- "Please do not tell the assistant personal details. An officer will ask you for what they need."
- "You can speak to a person at any time. Say 'officer' or press the key for an officer."
- "If you tell the assistant you or someone else is in danger, it will give you the right emergency number and transfer you to a person."
- "We review how the assistant is working every week and look at what people say about it."
- "If you have a concern, you can complain. [link]"
